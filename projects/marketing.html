Marketing KPI Demonstration

Joins sample campaign, spend and conversion tables, validates their grain, and compares efficiency metrics with an overall weighted ROAS calculation.

Download notebook ↓View repository ↗

Business question

How do campaign efficiency metrics compare in a small, inspectable example?

Approach

Join three sample tables—campaigns, spend and conversions—using validated one-to-one keys. Calculate CTR, CPC, conversion rate, CPA and ROAS, and compare campaign-level metrics.

Findings in the sample

  • Four campaign records total 9,000 in spend and 43,500 in revenue.
  • Overall ROAS is 4.833, calculated as total revenue divided by total spend.
  • The unweighted mean campaign ROAS is 4.628; it answers a different question.
  • Holiday Deal has the highest campaign ROAS (5.625), while Spring Sale has the lowest CPA (10).

Limits

These numbers are illustrative data constructed in Python. They do not describe actual brand performance, a deployed production pipeline or a measured business improvement. ROAS is not profit ROI, and real budget decisions require attribution, margin and incrementality information.
