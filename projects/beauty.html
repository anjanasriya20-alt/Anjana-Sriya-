Academic research paper

AI Adoption in Beauty Retail

Investigates consumer awareness, trust and barriers to AI adoption through survey analysis and comparative business research.

Download research PDF ↓View repository ↗

Research question

How do consumers perceive AI-enabled beauty shopping, and how do Nykaa and Sephora approach AI adoption?

Approach

Combine comparative business research with survey responses from 115 participants. Examine awareness, trust, privacy, use of AI features and adoption barriers; interpret the paper’s descriptive tables and statistical tests.

What the evidence shows

The survey table identifies high cost as the most frequently selected adoption barrier (38.3%); privacy remains a concern but should not be presented as the largest barrier in that table. The reported chi-square test has p=0.484, so it does not support an association at the 5% significance level. The reported regression models also have weak explanatory power and non-significant overall tests.

Business interpretation

Use the findings to frame questions about affordability, awareness and consumer confidence. Communicate uncertainty and distinguish responses in this survey from market-wide evidence.

Limits

The survey is small and predominantly student-based. It does not establish causal effects of AI or represent all beauty shoppers. This page summarizes the original academic paper; the complete PDF is available for inspection.
