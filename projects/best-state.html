Academic portfolio project

US State Comparison Dashboard

A multi-sheet workbook comparing education, housing, healthcare and other dimensions, with data transformation and automated scoring.

Download Excel workbook ↓View repository ↗

Business question

How do ten selected US states compare across education, crime, diversity, employment, demographics, housing and healthcare?

Approach

  • Bring together multiple dimensions in an Excel workbook and use Power Query for data preparation.
  • Automate scoring and formatting with VBA.
  • Compare indicators and examine how income, rent and employment enter the affordability view.

Preview rendered from the original workbook’s Education worksheet. Download the workbook to use its dashboard navigation and supporting sheets.

Deliverable

The original macro-enabled workbook is available for inspection. Its dashboard and supporting sheets expose the analysis structure; this page does not present a universal ranking of all US states.

Interpretation and limits

Composite rankings depend on source dates, indicator definitions and weights. Compare individual dimensions and test weighting choices before deciding where to live or invest. This project is a decision-support exercise, not a personalized location recommendation.
