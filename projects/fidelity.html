Academic client project · In progress

Fidelity Labs — Private Fund Data

Reviewing private-fund disclosures, assessing classification features, and prototyping an evidence-linked extraction workflow for the AltIQ practicum.

View repository ↗

Business problem

Private-fund information is spread across differently structured PPMs and disclosures. Comparable fields and clear category definitions are needed before deeper fund analysis can be reliable. The UMass Amherst practicum supports Fidelity Labs’ AltIQ exploration of alternative-investment intelligence for advisors and investors.

My contribution

  • Review investment objectives, underlying assets, security types, use of proceeds, return sources, risk, liquidity and management information.
  • Organize source-supported fields and preserve exact passages and page references for review.
  • Evaluate features that distinguish Private Equity, Private Credit and Private Real Assets, including ownership versus lending exposure.
  • Document inconsistent terminology, missing fields, limited training data and strategies that overlap categories.
  • Explore a Python-assisted extraction and provisional rule-based classification workflow, with human review of ambiguous results.

Approach

  1. Understand the business question and agree on category definitions.
  2. Review PDF/HTML disclosures and collect source evidence.
  3. Standardize meaningful features while distinguishing absence from uncertainty.
  4. Evaluate provisional strategy rules and flag mixed or ambiguous cases.
  5. Recommend data requirements and a repeatable review procedure.

Current outputs and next steps

The work has clarified extraction challenges, feature-selection needs and the importance of evidence-linked review. The Python workflow is a prototype; automated labels are provisional. This page does not claim a trained production classifier, validated accuracy or measured time savings.

Project context
