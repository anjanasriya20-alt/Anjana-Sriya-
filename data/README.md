# Data provenance
- `nyc-borough-summary.csv` and `nyc-roomtype-summary.csv`: borough-level and citywide room-type aggregates from the original portfolio project's historical NYC listing analysis. Collection date was not documented. Individual records and source identifiers are not included. The notebook records preparation filters and sample counts. Calendar availability is not observed occupancy.
- `target-*.csv`: chart traces recovered from the original committed notebook outputs, not the raw 47-column source. Market counts show the top 15 only; the map extract contains 1,829 historical store records.
- `marketing-sample.csv`: four illustrative campaign records constructed in Python; no client performance claims.
- `material-planning.json` and `material-plan.csv`: checked SQLite output from synthetic records; not client data.
- Laptop source: publisher-distributed mlba 2.0.5 teaching dataset, not copied into this repository.
